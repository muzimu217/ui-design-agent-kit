# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React + TypeScript, chosen for a runnable standalone product surface.

## Users

Primary users are shoppers comparing and configuring a premium smartphone before purchase. They are likely browsing on a phone or laptop and need to understand the product, choose a color and storage tier, and act with confidence.

## Product Purpose

FORMA One is a fictional smartphone product page that makes a phone configuration and purchase decision feel clear, tactile, and immediate. Success means a first-time visitor can identify the phone, understand its core value, compare the available choices, see the price update, and add a configuration to a shopping bag.

## Positioning

The page treats configuration as the product story: every choice updates the physical phone rendering, price, and order summary in one continuous surface. This is an illustrative concept, not a live commerce integration.

## Operating Context

The experience is a responsive web product surface for mobile-first browsing, laptop comparison, and keyboard-accessible selection. It is evaluated locally with fixture product data and no backend persistence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Display a hero product rendering without relying on remote media.
- Allow color and storage selection with visible selected states.
- Update the displayed price and capacity copy from the selected configuration.
- Provide an add-to-bag action with a reversible, visible confirmation state.
- Include specifications and product highlights with progressive disclosure.
- Support 375px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px widths without horizontal overflow.
- Respect keyboard focus, semantic controls, and prefers-reduced-motion.
- Product, price, reviews, inventory, and customer claims are illustrative demo data and must not be presented as live proof.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is FORMA One. The visual identity is intentionally new and independent from the parent UI Design Agent showcase. Product copy may use concise Chinese with English product labels for a coherent bilingual retail tone.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer, benchmark, review, or production commerce evidence was supplied. The phone rendering is authored in CSS/HTML and is illustrative.

## Product Principles

1. Make the physical object inspectable immediately.
2. Keep configuration choices adjacent to their consequences.
3. Use material and motion to clarify state, never to obscure it.
4. Make the purchase action obvious, reversible, and honest about demo status.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native buttons and disclosure controls, visible focus styling, touch targets of at least 44px, non-color-only selected states, and a reduced-motion mode that removes nonessential movement.

## Open Decisions

Audience, value proposition, product proof, and stack are inferred from the explicit user brief; no live checkout, account, analytics, or external asset source is part of this concept.
